SoHo Lofts

A residential loft concept framed around soft daylight, refined materials, and the calm pace of downtown interior living in SoHo. Our job was to capture the silent luxury of cast-iron architecture, recreating the specific way light passes through the classic high loft windows.

Chasing the light

Capturing the soft morning sun

In a SoHo loft, light is everything. We modeled the tall, historic cast-iron windows and the buildings across the street to simulate exactly how the light falls in during different hours of the day. The result is a series of stills that show the home at its quietest, most inviting moment.

Materiality first

Honest textures, rendered with absolute precision

From the soft grain of the timber floors to the textured plaster walls, every finish was selected to create a sense of tactile warmth. We spend hours refining our material maps so that when you look at a rendering, you don't just see a room — you feel it.
