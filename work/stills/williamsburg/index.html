Williamsburg

Located on Wythe Ave, Williamsburg, one of Brooklyn's most vibrant neighborhoods, a residential project framed around soft daylight and industrial brick elements. Our work brings the project into its context with high-fidelity, photorealistic details that capture the essence of waterfront living.

Neighborhood Integration

Framed to capture the true streetscape

We modeled the street corridor including the adjacent storefronts, trees, and sidewalks to place the project directly within the fabric of Williamsburg. It isn't just about rendering the building's facade, it is about illustrating how the structure coexists with its neighbors under the Brooklyn sky.

Architectural Clarity

Highlighting raw textures and daylight

Brick, metal, and glass. We paid special attention to standardizing the material values so they reflect light exactly as real brick and anodized aluminum do. The soft daylight enhances the facade, establishing an editorial presentation of the architectural craftsmanship.
